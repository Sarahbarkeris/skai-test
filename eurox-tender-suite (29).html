PQQ vs ITT — what's the difference?
A PQQ (Pre-Qualification Questionnaire) is Stage 1 — the buyer screens suppliers on capability, certifications, and experience. No pricing submitted here. Pass and you move to Stage 2.

An ITT (Invitation to Tender) is Stage 2 — full submission including detailed answers and your commercial pricing. Both stages tracked separately with their own deadlines. When a PQQ outcome is logged, the system prompts you to create the ITT record.
